# Klea - Recovery Companion App

Klea is a private recovery journaling app designed for patients navigating the emotional and physical aspects of healing.

## Overview

Capturing the full recovery journey, not just the medical one. Klea emphasizes emotional recovery by helping patients find new perspectives during the recovery process.

## Key Features

### Recovery Pathways
- Post-surgery recovery
- Cancer treatment
- Injury/trauma recovery
- Autoimmune flare management
- Cardiac event recovery
- Long hospital stays
- High-risk pregnancy support
- Undiagnosed/mystery illness support

### Core Functionality
- **Video Journaling (Vlogging)**: Record your recovery journey
- **Written Journaling**: Traditional journal entries
- **Photo Documentation**: Visual symptom tracking
- **Symptom Tracking**: Monitor recovery with multimedia support
- **Reflection Prompts**: Condition-specific, emotional support-focused prompts
- **Calendar View**: Swipeable symptom history (inspired by BeReal)

## Symptom Tracking Categories
- Mobility
- Appetite
- Overall mood
- Motivation
- Energy levels
- Pain levels
- Sleep quality
- Other custom symptoms

## Design Principles
- **Easy to Use**: Intuitive interface for patients unfamiliar with technology or recovering state
- **Clear Reflection**: Focused prompts that encourage meaningful self-reflection
- **Emotional Support First**: Comfort-focused rather than clinical health management
- **Privacy**: All data remains private and secure
