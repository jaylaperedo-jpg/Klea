# Klea Color Scheme

A calm, relaxing palette designed to support patients through their recovery journey. Emphasizing serenity and emotional comfort.

## Primary Colors

### Light Blue Palette (Primary)
- **Soft Sky Blue**: `#E8F4F8` - Main backgrounds, cards, gentle surfaces
- **Light Periwinkle**: `#D4E6F1` - Secondary backgrounds, subtle accents
- **Serene Blue**: `#A9CCE3` - Interactive elements, focus states
- **Calm Teal**: `#85C1E2` - Buttons, important actions
- **Deep Meditation Blue**: `#5DADE2` - Text links, secondary headers

### Neutral/Base
- **Pure White**: `#FFFFFF` - Primary text, clean spaces
- **Soft White**: `#F9FBFC` - Light backgrounds, breathing room
- **Cool Gray**: `#BDC3C7` - Borders, dividers, subtle elements
- **Dark Navy**: `#1C2E3E` - Primary text, headers

### Accent - Warm Yellow
- **Warm Sunshine Yellow**: `#FDB927` - Highlights, encouragement, milestones
- **Soft Golden Yellow**: `#FFE082` - Gentle highlights, notifications
- **Light Cream**: `#FFFBEA` - Warm background accents

### Status Colors
- **Success/Recovery**: `#52BE80` (soft green) - Milestones achieved, positive progress
- **Gentle Warning**: `#F8B88B` (soft orange) - Symptom concerns, needs attention
- **Info/Support**: `#A9CCE3` (light blue) - Tips, prompts, guidance

## Usage Guidelines

### Primary UI Elements
- **Backgrounds**: Soft Sky Blue (`#E8F4F8`), Soft White (`#F9FBFC`)
- **Cards/Containers**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`) borders
- **Headers**: Dark Navy (`#1C2E3E`) text on light backgrounds
- **Buttons**: Calm Teal (`#85C1E2`) with white text for primary actions
- **Secondary Buttons**: Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`) with Dark Navy text

### Interactive Elements
- **Focus States**: Serene Blue (`#A9CCE3`) border/glow
- **Hover States**: Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`)
- **Active States**: Calm Teal (`#85C1E2`)

### Accents & Highlights
- **Encouragement/Success**: Warm Sunshine Yellow (`#FDB927`)
- **Important Notifications**: Warm Sunshine Yellow with soft icons
- **Recovery Milestones**: Combine yellow with soft green

### Specific Page/Feature Colors

#### Reflection Prompt Cards
- **Background**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Border**: Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`)
- **Text**: Dark Navy (`#1C2E3E`)
- **Highlight**: Warm Sunshine Yellow (`#FDB927`) for encouragement elements

#### Symptom Tracker
- **Default**: Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`)
- **Mobility**: Calm Teal (`#85C1E2`)
- **Appetite**: Warm Golden Yellow (`#FFE082`)
- **Mood**: Serene Blue (`#A9CCE3`)
- **Motivation**: Warm Sunshine Yellow (`#FDB927`)

#### Calendar View (History)
- **Background**: Soft Sky Blue (`#E8F4F8`)
- **Day Cards**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Selected Day**: Light Periwinkle (`#D4E6F1`)
- **Photo Thumbnails**: Soft border in Serene Blue (`#A9CCE3`)

#### Journal Screens
- **Background**: Soft White (`#F9FBFC`)
- **Writing Area**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Recording Indicator**: Warm Sunshine Yellow (`#FDB927`)

#### Settings Page
- **Background**: Soft Sky Blue (`#E8F4F8`)
- **Toggle On**: Calm Teal (`#85C1E2`)
- **Toggle Off**: Cool Gray (`#BDC3C7`)
- **Delete/Destructive**: Soft Warning (`#F8B88B`)

## Accessibility

- **Contrast Ratios**: All text colors meet WCAG AA standards (4.5:1 minimum)
- **Color-Blind Friendly**: Palette tested for deuteranopia (red-green), protanopia (red-blind), and tritanopia (blue-yellow)
- **Large Text**: Headers use Dark Navy for maximum readability
- **Focus Indicators**: Serene Blue borders ensure keyboard navigation visibility

## Typography Colors

- **Primary Text**: Dark Navy (`#1C2E3E`)
- **Secondary Text**: Deep Meditation Blue (`#5DADE2`)
- **Placeholder/Hint Text**: Cool Gray (`#BDC3C7`)
- **Links**: Deep Meditation Blue (`#5DADE2`) - underlined
- **Success Messages**: Soft Green (`#52BE80`)
- **Error Messages**: Soft Warning (`#F8B88B`)

## Visual Design Philosophy

The color scheme creates an environment that feels:
- **Calm & Peaceful**: Dominated by soft blues and whites
- **Supportive & Warm**: Yellow accents provide hope and encouragement
- **Safe & Private**: Cool tones convey trust and security
- **Accessible**: High contrast and warm neutrals ensure readability
- **Healing-Focused**: Serene palette supports emotional recovery
